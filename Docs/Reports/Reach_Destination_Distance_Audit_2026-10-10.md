# Reach destination and distance audit

Date requested: 2026-10-10. Inspection performed: 2026-10-11. Scope: source and data inspection only. No route, runtime, test, configuration, or save changes were made. All paths below are repository-relative; line numbers refer to this checkout. "Verified" means established by source or the checked-in data asset, not a visual acceptance or a live screensaver run.

## Executive summary and counts

- **Verified:** Reach is a fixed, repeating 41-stop itinerary: all 37 authored Solar System natural bodies and four of twelve spacecraft. Its constructor resolves every stop only through `Universe.get()` in the current `c0` system; departure increments an array index and camera cruise uses a body target. It has no interstellar or point-target stop today. [src/reach/ReachVoyageDirector.ts:18-60](../../src/reach/ReachVoyageDirector.ts#L18), [src/reach/ReachVoyageDirector.ts:242-297](../../src/reach/ReachVoyageDirector.ts#L242), [src/reach/ReachVoyageDirector.ts:372-375](../../src/reach/ReachVoyageDirector.ts#L372).
- **Verified:** The authored explorer inventory is 37 natural Solar System bodies, 12 spacecraft, 26 non-pulsar landmarks (7 nebulae, 8 clusters, 7 remnants, 4 stars), 9 pulsars, 14 black-hole entries, and 55 labelled galaxies including the Milky Way. The nine pulsars are spread into `LANDMARKS`, so the runtime landmark count is 35; they must not be counted twice. These are catalogue entries, not a count of physically distinct astronomical objects: galaxy cores, embedded stars and remnants can be represented twice. [src/data/solarSystem.ts:42-45](../../src/data/solarSystem.ts#L42), [src/data/solarSystem.ts:394-405](../../src/data/solarSystem.ts#L394), [src/data/spacecraft.ts:33-36](../../src/data/spacecraft.ts#L33), [src/data/landmarks.ts:69-72](../../src/data/landmarks.ts#L69), [src/data/landmarks.ts:239-240](../../src/data/landmarks.ts#L239), [src/data/pulsars.ts:91-93](../../src/data/pulsars.ts#L91), [src/data/blackholes.ts:224-226](../../src/data/blackholes.ts#L224), [src/galaxy/galaxies.ts:16-17](../../src/galaxy/galaxies.ts#L16), [src/galaxy/galaxies.ts:139-149](../../src/galaxy/galaxies.ts#L139).
- **Verified:** The checked-in HYG-derived `public/data/stars.bin` header reports 109,401 stars. `public/data/star-names.json` has 6,552 designation rows, of which 460 have a non-Sun proper name. The parser uses `c{index}` IDs and proper/Bayer/Gliese/HIP fallbacks. They are *bulk catalogue* stars with generated planetary systems, not 460 hand-authored destinations. The galaxy index has 54 authored external galaxies plus `g0` Milky Way and defaults to 110,000 synthetic galaxies. Far tiers contain 260,000 and 160,000 aggregate points; they are visual structure, not named galaxies. [src/data/StarCatalog.ts:49-67](../../src/data/StarCatalog.ts#L49), [src/data/StarCatalog.ts:69-117](../../src/data/StarCatalog.ts#L69), [src/data/StarCatalog.ts:130-139](../../src/data/StarCatalog.ts#L130), [src/galaxy/galaxies.ts:115-149](../../src/galaxy/galaxies.ts#L115), [src/galaxy/FarUniverse.ts:37-60](../../src/galaxy/FarUniverse.ts#L37).
- **Inference:** Distance-aware routing is feasible if it operates on typed destination references and Sun-centred scene-frame positions in parsecs, evaluates moving body positions at a defined Julian date, and treats visual quality and duplicate identity as explicit eligibility fields. It needs new Reach travel and persistence support before any non-`c0` stop can be certified. [src/core/Universe.ts:267-298](../../src/core/Universe.ts#L267), [src/core/Universe.ts:328-357](../../src/core/Universe.ts#L328), [src/main.ts:312-370](../../src/main.ts#L312).

These are source-based readiness labels; `B` is not a screensaver visual approval.

## Coordinates and transit distance

| Source | Position/frame and accuracy boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Solar bodies and spacecraft | System-local km, hierarchical parent orbits, evaluated at Julian date. JPL-style major-planet elements and simpler moon/minor-body approximations coexist. Spacecraft positions are explicitly approximate; radial probe propagation is not a full Horizons ephemeris. [src/data/solarSystem.ts:10-34](../../src/data/solarSystem.ts#L10), [src/data/spacecraft.ts:5-28](../../src/data/spacecraft.ts#L5), [src/core/CelestialBody.ts:156-176](../../src/core/CelestialBody.ts#L156). |
| HYG stars | Sun-relative scene Cartesian parsecs as float32 from `stars.bin`. `Universe.starPositionPc()` can convert any catalogue or generated star to this frame. [src/data/StarCatalog.ts:26-28](../../src/data/StarCatalog.ts#L26), [src/data/StarCatalog.ts:85-90](../../src/data/StarCatalog.ts#L85), [src/core/Universe.ts:267-273](../../src/core/Universe.ts#L267). |
| Landmarks/pulsars | Explicit Sun-distance pc along `raDecToScene()` or a model galaxy disc-plane intersection. The latter is an estimate clamped to 0.8-1.2 times the galaxy distance. [src/core/Universe.ts:138-165](../../src/core/Universe.ts#L138). |
| Black holes | HYG host, model Milky Way/galaxy centre, explicit RA/Dec times pc, or host disc-plane estimate. Galaxy-name lookup falls back to zero vector for some unresolved hosts; validation must reject that fallback for routing. [src/data/blackholes.ts:123-161](../../src/data/blackholes.ts#L123), [src/data/blackholes.ts:189-220](../../src/data/blackholes.ts#L189), [src/core/Universe.ts:110-136](../../src/core/Universe.ts#L110). |
| Galaxies | Sun-relative scene Cartesian parsecs stored in float32, derived from authored RA/Dec and Mpc distance. Mpc-to-pc conversion is direct; model disc normal comes from sky position angle and inclination. Synthetic galaxy positions are model output. [src/galaxy/galaxies.ts:168-180](../../src/galaxy/galaxies.ts#L168), [src/galaxy/frames.ts:46-66](../../src/galaxy/frames.ts#L46). |
| Frame conversion | ICRF RA/Dec is rotated to the ecliptic and then into Y-up scene coordinates. Galactic coordinates have an explicit basis. Every `StarSystem.origin` is Sun-relative km; `Universe.switchTo()` translates camera coordinates, while `toParsecs()` returns Sun-relative parsecs. [src/math/frames.ts:5-34](../../src/math/frames.ts#L5), [src/galaxy/frames.ts:5-23](../../src/galaxy/frames.ts#L5), [src/core/StarSystem.ts:6-24](../../src/core/StarSystem.ts#L6), [src/core/Universe.ts:328-356](../../src/core/Universe.ts#L328). |

**Recommended calculation, inferred from those source contracts:** Resolve each stop centre at the same declared transit epoch into Sun-centred scene-frame km: `system.origin + body.position` for a body; `starPositionPc * PARSEC_KM` for a star; `landmark.positionPc * PARSEC_KM` for a landmark; `blackHole.positionPc * PARSEC_KM + local hole offset` for a hole; and `galaxies.position(i) * PARSEC_KM` for a galaxy centre. Then use Euclidean norm of destination minus origin. For large separations, calculate in parsecs first to reduce large-km cancellation, converting only for display; for Solar System legs, use km directly. AU, LY and Mly use [src/core/constants.ts:1-4](../../src/core/constants.ts#L1). This gives a geometric snapshot separation, not spacecraft path length, light-travel time, or cosmological proper distance. For moving bodies, choose and record departure epoch, arrival epoch or both; the current route is time-advanced during cruise. [src/reach/ReachVoyageDirector.ts:300-306](../../src/reach/ReachVoyageDirector.ts#L300).

**Limit:** Galactic and ICRF directions are transformed by source utilities, but there is no evidenced cosmological comoving-to-proper or redshift/time-dependent transform. Far-tier shell positions are illustrative comoving-scale aggregates; treating them as precise pairwise transit distances would be unsupported. Float32 galaxy coordinates and approximate galaxy/landmark distances also limit high-precision claims. [src/galaxy/FarUniverse.ts:37-63](../../src/galaxy/FarUniverse.ts#L37), [src/galaxy/galaxies.ts:122-149](../../src/galaxy/galaxies.ts#L122).

## Blockers, duplicates and visual cautions

1. **Reach target model:** `body()` only calls `Universe.get()` in the active system. Cross-system stars/black holes need `switchTo()` and a camera frame shift; nebulae and galaxies need `goToPoint()` plus an anchor. The saved flight union only represents a body `goto` and demands `referenceId === targetId === destinationId`. [src/reach/ReachVoyageDirector.ts:123-179](../../src/reach/ReachVoyageDirector.ts#L123), [src/reach/JourneyState.ts:85-117](../../src/reach/JourneyState.ts#L85), [src/camera/CameraController.ts:236-251](../../src/camera/CameraController.ts#L236).
2. **Repeated objects:** `g0` Milky Way versus `sun`/`c0`; `g25` versus `b1` M87*; M31/M81/Sombrero/M106/Centaurus A galaxies versus their core black holes; Crab nebula versus Crab pulsar; Tarantula versus R136a1; galaxy centres versus embedded clusters/stars; HYG named stars versus landmark stars may also overlap by physical identity and should be checked before route selection. These are inferred identity overlaps, not confirmed catalogue de-duplication. [src/data/landmarks.ts:72-97](../../src/data/landmarks.ts#L72), [src/data/pulsars.ts:91-95](../../src/data/pulsars.ts#L91), [src/data/blackholes.ts:257-280](../../src/data/blackholes.ts#L257), [src/data/blackholes.ts:427-464](../../src/data/blackholes.ts#L427).
3. **Visual variety:** Seven extended landmarks lack photographs; most galaxies use analytic morphology, and many HYG systems use generated planets. Four outbound probes share two model families; New Horizons and Curiosity are explicitly representative. A large adjacent run of generic sprites, generated stars, dwarf spheroidals or similar spiral galaxies is likely repetitive. This is a design inference; no Game-view or screensaver visual review was performed. [src/data/landmarkImages.ts:15-29](../../src/data/landmarkImages.ts#L15), [src/render/GalaxySprites.ts:15-33](../../src/render/GalaxySprites.ts#L15), [src/core/Universe.ts:295-313](../../src/core/Universe.ts#L295), [src/data/spacecraft.ts:35-72](../../src/data/spacecraft.ts#L35).
4. **Position quality:** HYG coordinates are float32; Milky Way centre is an 8.2 kpc model; external landmark depth often uses a clamped disc-plane estimate; spacecraft use approximate or simplified motion. Distances should be labelled model geometric estimates, especially outside the Solar System. [src/data/StarCatalog.ts:55-56](../../src/data/StarCatalog.ts#L55), [src/galaxy/frames.ts:13-15](../../src/galaxy/frames.ts#L13), [src/core/Universe.ts:153-165](../../src/core/Universe.ts#L153), [src/data/spacecraft.ts:5-28](../../src/data/spacecraft.ts#L5).
5. **Catalogue resolution:** Cygnus X-1 depends on `catalog.search('HIP 98298', 1)` and is skipped if absent. The host-galaxy lookup for external landmarks/holes uses name substrings and may skip a landmark or return origin fallback for a hole if a name stops matching. A typed route should validate resolved targets before admitting them. [src/core/Universe.ts:106-148](../../src/core/Universe.ts#L106).
6. **Distant limit:** No hand-authored named structure beyond Antennae at 22 Mpc is present in `CATALOG`; far-tier aggregates are generated visual points, not verified destination objects. ## Minimum viable routing architecture and test impact

Recommendation only: define a small versioned registry of **typed, curated** stop refs (`body` with system ID/body ID, `star`, `landmark`, `blackHole`, `galaxy`) with display label, grouping, position resolver, arrival/observation strategy, visual eligibility and optional `samePhysicalObject` key. Use the existing Universe frame utilities to resolve positions at the chosen epoch. Rank eligible candidates within broad bands by actual 3D pairwise distance and variety, and explicitly preserve scale transitions; do not pick from all HYG/procedural entries. Store chosen stable ref/leg endpoints and completion state so the next stop and cumulative distance survive reload. Keep route selection separate from camera presentation. This is an inferred design, not implemented behavior. The current explorer APIs demonstrate the target strategies: [src/main.ts:312-370](../../src/main.ts#L312), [src/main.ts:422-436](../../src/main.ts#L422), [src/core/Universe.ts:328-357](../../src/core/Universe.ts#L328).

The existing `scripts/test-reach-journey.mjs` asserts exactly 41 stops, Solar-only fixtures, Earth start, four current timing categories, body-flight save/restore continuity, phase countdowns, panorama primary/follower behavior, and legacy timing preservation. A distance-aware route would need fixtures for deterministic candidate choice/tie-breaking, moving-body epoch and cross-class frame conversions, fixed-point observation, interstellar frame switch, galaxy-centre arrival, duplicate suppression, no double-count on resume, and invalid-target fallback. Update or replace the 41-stop and `c0`-only assertions after the route contract is decided. Current parser checks itinerary identity but does not require `route[routeIndex].id === destinationId`; the test deliberately accepts a reordered index then restores by ID. A dynamic route must persist/validate chosen next leg or a route snapshot rather than trust index alone.
